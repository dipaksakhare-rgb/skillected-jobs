Privacy Policy

Minimal collection

We collect only what the platform needs to operate: account email (for candidates and staff), application click counts without personal identifiers, and resume content you explicitly upload for matching (Phase 4).

Resumes are sensitive

Uploaded resumes are stored privately, never displayed publicly, never sold, and can be deleted by you at any time. We apply a documented retention policy.

Click & QR analytics

Apply clicks and QR scans are logged with job id, timestamp and coarse device class only — no personal profiles.

Your rights

You can request account deletion and resume deletion at any time. Consent is requested before any resume processing.

Contact

Reach the Skillected team via skillected.com.
